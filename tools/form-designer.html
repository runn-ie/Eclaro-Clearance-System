Fields toolbox

1. Choose a field type.
2. Right-click and drag on the document where the field belongs.
3. Select a placed field to edit it. Drag to move, use the corner to resize.
4. Select a cell box to drag the green handles and move its column and row lines.
5. Press Test fields to fill in the form and see the live preview of the file.

Your uploaded document stays visible underneath the fields while you map the form.
